# FlightSweeper demo recording guide

Use `narration.mp3` as the voice track. Its paragraphs correspond to these screen beats:

1. Hero and sandbox disclosure.
2. Active LAX to JFK mandate.
3. ChatGPT reads the mission and searches.
4. Adversarial offer, denial, and denial receipt.
5. Tighten the mission to nonstop.
6. Select Coast Air, refresh, and evaluate.
7. Purchase and reveal the sandbox ticket receipt.
8. Replay the purchase, revoke authority, and reload.
9. End on the visible evidence or repository page.

Keep pointer movement deliberate and avoid long pauses. Leave the product audio muted while recording, add this narration track in the editor, and export at 1080p. The final video must remain under three minutes.
