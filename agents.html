Agent access

FlightSweeper Challenge Edition is a deterministic synthetic sandbox. Compatible browser agents can discover all 10 WebMCP tools. FlightSweeper permits only actions that are valid for the current mission state. Remote agents can inspect four read-only capabilities at https://webmcp.flightsweeper.com/mcp.

Safety boundary

No supplier, payment, passenger identity, reservation, or ticketing system is connected. The sandbox cannot purchase real travel. Authority may only tighten, completed actions replay their original synthetic receipt, and revocation blocks future actions.
